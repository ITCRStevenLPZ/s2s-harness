    Criteria cheat sheet
    Naturalness / Engagement > Utility > Audio Quality

    Utility and Audio Quality carry less weight, but both must clear a basic threshold: no misinformation, no hallucination, no jarring audio errors. Naturalness only carries its full weight once both clips clear that bar.

    The four dimensions

    • Naturalness / Engagement / Aesthetics — which clip sounded more human, expressive and warm? Score robotic intonation and unnatural rhythm lower.
    • Utility — which response was genuinely more useful, practical and grounded in real facts?
    • Audio Quality — which had greater clarity, without distortion, clicks, pops or noticeable cutoffs?
    • Overall Preference — all things considered, which clip do you prefer?
    Audio tolerance. Do not penalize light background noise that sounds like a normal room or office (slight room impulse), or a faint "air" sound, as long as it is not jarring. State in the justification whether you penalized Audio Quality or not.
    Never "Both Good" on Audio when one clip has noticeable background noise, clicks, cutoffs or similar. Listen to each clip in isolation on stereo headphones.

    Issues to flag explicitly

    • Repetitive words or phrases
    • A response that degrades as it goes on
    • Awkward silence / failing to respond
    • Embodied-experience claims — a digital AI offering to do something physical
    • Refusal or failure to follow the instruction
    Errors do not automatically lose. A clip with one of these errors can still be the better pick if it is meaningfully more natural — but you must name the error and explain the trade-off with evidence.
    Read the prompt type. When the prompt contains a false premise, a clip that disagrees with it is succeeding, not refusing to help.

    What a strong justification contains

    1. A clear overall preference
    2. Which level of the hierarchy drove it, and why
    3. Specific, observable evidence from both clips
    4. An explicit statement about threshold issues observed or not observed
    5. Any error clusters, named
